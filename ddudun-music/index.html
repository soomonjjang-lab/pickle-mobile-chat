Christmas Special is eight carols — warm, bright, a duet, a rap, one with a distorted guitar. Every song plays free, with the full English lyric on screen.

Where to start

If it’s December

「Christmas on Our Street」 for an easy, bright carol; 「Meet Me Under the Lights」 if you want the duet; 「Christmas in My Heart」 when you want the loud, guitar-first one.

If it’s 2 a.m.

「What I Meant to Say」 and 「By the Time I Knew」 — the pair the album is built around. 「Always Right There」 if you have five minutes and something to sit with.

If you want the words

The lyric player shows every line in English with a Korean translation beside it, and jumps to that moment in the song when you tap a line.

How these songs are made. The lyrics, the direction — the mood, the voice, the arrangement, the story of each song — are written and decided by the artist. Vocals and instruments are generated with Suno and then produced into the tracks here; the images in the videos are AI-generated as well, and each video carries YouTube’s “altered or synthetic content” label. Nothing here is a cover or a sample of another artist’s recording.
