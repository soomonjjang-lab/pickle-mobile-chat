    65W GaN 3-Port USB-C Charger

    For a bag that carries a laptop, a phone and one cable too many.

    • A built-in USB-C cable plus two more ports, so a laptop, a phone and earbuds can share one wall outlet.
    • GaN internals keep it smaller than the brick that came with most laptops, and the prongs fold flat for a bag.
    • The listing gives 65 W as a shared total across the three ports, with US, EU and Korea plug options.

    Check which plug is in the box — the same listing offers US and EU plugs as well as the Korea one — and whether the built-in cable is retractable on the version you pick.
