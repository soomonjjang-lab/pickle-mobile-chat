✈️Flights

Dates and flights come first. Searching is free; the payment happens on the airline's or the seller's own site.

  • Aviasales

    Compares airline and ticket-agency fares for the same route on one screen.

    • Enter an origin, a destination and dates; fares from several sellers appear side by side.
    • If your dates are flexible, the month view shows which days around the trip are cheaper.
    • The cheapest fare is usually the least flexible one — long layovers, no changes, no refund.

    CheckBefore paying, check who issues the ticket (the airline itself or an agency), whether checked bags are included, and the change and refund rules. With an agency-issued ticket, that agency is who you call when a flight is disrupted.

    Search flights↗

  • Kiwi.com

    Searches and sells itineraries that stitch several airlines into one trip.

    • Builds 'self-transfer' itineraries out of separate airlines, which often costs less than a direct flight or an official connection.
    • Mixing low-cost carriers in makes short-haul trips, especially within Europe, noticeably cheaper.
    • Connection protection is an add-on: without it, a missed connection is yours to solve, not the airline's.

    CheckCheck whether the connection is a self-transfer and whether you have to collect and re-check your bags. On tight connections checked bags may not be offered at all, and if an earlier flight is late the next leg is your cost. Add connection protection at booking if you want cover.

    Search combined flights↗

📶eSIM · Data

Install before you fly, so you are not queueing for a SIM after landing. Your phone needs eSIM support and no carrier lock.

  • Airalo

    Buys and installs a travel data eSIM from an app, before or after landing.

    • Country, regional and global plans, so a single-country trip and a multi-country one both fit.
    • Installation takes a few minutes, by QR code or inside the app.
    • Most plans are data-only, so anything that needs a phone number has to be handled separately.

    CheckYour phone must support eSIM and be carrier-unlocked. Check the data amount, the validity period, and whether hotspot/tethering works on that plan.

    See eSIM plans↗

  • Yesim

    A second eSIM option, with data and voice plans in the same app.

    • Country and regional eSIMs, plus plans that include calls when you need a number.
    • Worth comparing on long stays and business trips, where the cost per day matters more.
    • Buying, installing and managing all happen in the app.

    CheckCheck the supported device list and that your phone is unlocked before buying. Two plans for the same country can differ in data, duration and speed limits.

    See Yesim plans↗

  • Saily

    A travel eSIM from Nord Security, the company behind NordVPN; you buy and install the plan in the app.

    • Country, regional and global plans across 200+ destinations.
    • Install from the app, then switch the plan on after landing — the validity starts when you activate it.
    • If you need a number for calls, a US (+1) line is an optional monthly add-on.

    CheckYour phone must support eSIM and be carrier-unlocked. Data amount and validity differ per plan; installing early is fine, because the clock starts only when you turn it on at your destination.

    See Saily plans↗

  • GigSky

    A travel eSIM that also covers cruise ships and in-flight data, not only land networks.

    • Alongside country and regional plans, it sells separate cruise, inflight and offshore plans.
    • A cruise plan starts once the ship is about 14 miles (22 km) offshore and connects to the maritime network.
    • An inflight plan switches on above 20,000 feet on participating airlines.

    CheckCruise and inflight plans work only on supported ships and airlines, so check those lists first. At sea, turn the ship's Wi-Fi off or the eSIM data is not used. The phone must support eSIM and be carrier-unlocked.

    See GigSky plans↗

🚐Airport transfer

For a late arrival or too many bags, a driver already booked is the least tiring way in.

  • Kiwitaxi

    Pre-books an airport pickup with a driver waiting at your arrival time.

    • You enter the flight number, and the pickup is timed to the arrival.
    • The price is set when you book, rather than running on a meter to the door.
    • Choose the vehicle class to match passengers and luggage.

    CheckCheck the free waiting time after landing, whether the driver meets you inside with a name sign, and the cancellation deadline.

    Book a transfer↗

  • GetTransfer

    Compares driver quotes for airport pickups and city-to-city rides.

    • Post the route and the time; drivers offer prices; you pick one.
    • Airport pickups and longer intercity runs are booked the same way.
    • Vehicle photos and driver ratings are shown before you choose.

    CheckCheck whether tolls, parking and night surcharges sit inside the quoted price, and keep the inclusions in writing in the booking chat.

    Compare transfer quotes↗

  • Welcome Pickups

    An airport pickup where the driver you booked waits at arrivals with a name sign.

    • The fare is fixed when you book; the driver meets you with a name board and walks you to the car.
    • Handpicked, English-speaking drivers in 374 destinations, and your flight number is tracked so a delay does not cost you the ride.
    • The same app books in-city rides and sightseeing rides with a driver for part of a day.

    CheckCheck whether tolls and parking sit inside the fare, how long the free waiting time is, and the cancellation deadline. Pick the vehicle size for your group and bags, and ask for a child seat when booking if you need one.

    Book airport pickup↗

🚗Car rental

For the trips where you take the car at the airport and drive yourself. Bookings are usually free to cancel; the deposit is often held on a card at the counter.

  • QEEQ

    A booking site that puts the same dates at many rental companies on one screen.

    • Airport counters and city offices appear together, and the total after insurance options is what you compare.
    • International brands and local operators sit side by side, so the deposit, the car's age and the return terms differ for the same class.
    • A free-cancellation label varies by product, so read it again before the payment screen.

    CheckCheck whether the shown rate includes insurance and the excess, whether mileage is unlimited, the fuel policy and the return time. Look up whether an international driving permit and a credit card for the deposit are needed, and which borders the car may cross.

    Compare car rental rates↗

  • Auto Europe

    A booking agent for European car hire since 1954, comparing major brands with local suppliers on the same terms.

    • Avis, Budget, Europcar and Hertz sit next to regional companies on the same terms, so the price is what separates them.
    • The booking is with the agent and the car is collected from the local office, so a problem has two places to call.
    • Motorhomes and cars with a driver are booked through the same desk.

    CheckCheck whether the pick-up on the voucher is an airport counter or an off-site office reached by shuttle, and how late it is open. Check whether winter tyres or chains, tolls and vignettes, and an extra-driver fee are inside the rate before you pay.

    See Auto Europe rates↗

  • Localrent

    A marketplace of local rental companies, with the most choice where the big brands are thin.

    • Instead of an international brand you see the country's own operators, which often means a cheaper car for the same days.
    • Many local operators have no airport counter; they bring the car to your hotel or the airport instead.
    • The deposit is often low, but the car's age and what the insurance actually covers differ sharply between operators.

    CheckRead the reviews for that operator and check the deposit (cash or a card hold), the excess limit and any mileage cap. Confirm the pick-up place and time in a message, and photograph and film the car at handover so the return is not an argument.

    See Localrent cars↗

🎟️Tours · Passes · Tickets

Tickets, tours and city passes in one place. Popular sights run out at the gate, so booking ahead is the safer plan.

  • Klook

    Books tours, attraction tickets and transport passes for a city in one app.

    • Deepest in major Asian cities — theme parks, observation decks, day tours — and covers other regions too.
    • Many bookings arrive as a mobile voucher you show at the gate.
    • Each product sets its own date rules and cancellation deadline, so read the page.

    CheckCheck whether your date and time slot are fixed, whether the booking is confirmed instantly or after review, and the cancellation deadline for that specific product.

    Browse tours & tickets↗

  • KKday

    An Asia-based booking platform for local tours, activities and airport pickups.

    • Lots of small-group tours and transport products across Asian and Pacific cities.
    • Sells guided tours and self-guided passes in the same catalogue.
    • Reviews and photos on each product page show how the day actually runs.

    CheckMeeting points and times differ by product. Check whether the tour needs a minimum number of guests to run, and whether pickup is included or extra.

    Browse KKday↗

  • Tiqets

    Reserves museum, gallery and exhibition tickets for a chosen date.

    • Centred on museums and galleries in major European cities, with more elsewhere.
    • Most tickets arrive as a QR code you show at the entrance.
    • Some venues require a time slot, not just a date.

    CheckThe venue's own ticket is sometimes cheaper, so compare before paying. Check whether entry is immediate or time-slotted, and the cancellation terms on that ticket.

    See tickets↗

  • Go City

    A multi-attraction city pass that covers several sights with one purchase.

    • Two shapes: a pass counted in days, and a pass counted in attractions.
    • The list of included sights differs by city.
    • The more you visit, the better it does against buying tickets one by one.

    CheckCheck that the sights you want are on that city's list, and whether any of them need a reservation. Passes start counting at first use, but the rule differs by pass.

    See city passes↗

  • WeGoTrip

    An entry ticket with an audio guide attached, walked at your own pace instead of on a fixed schedule.

    • No guide to keep up with: you listen in the app or from a link, starting, pausing and resuming as you like.
    • Some products include the admission ticket and some are the audio alone, so read what the specific tour includes.
    • Download the tour before you go and it plays without local data.

    CheckCheck the audio language, whether admission is included, and the validity (a fixed date or not). Bring earphones and a power bank, and re-check each museum's opening hours and closing day before booking.

    See audio tours↗

🧳Luggage storage

On the last day, the hours between checkout and the flight go better without the bags.

  • Radical Storage

    Finds and books luggage storage in shops and hotels, on a map.

    • Partner shops near stations and sights take bags by the hour or by the day.
    • For the gap between checkout and a flight, or the hours before check-in.
    • Book, then show the booking at the shop when you drop the bags.

    CheckCheck the shop's opening hours and what it will take — suitcase size, bikes, awkward items. Insurance and the rules for valuables follow that partner shop's terms.

The order to book in

Top to bottom is the whole preparation. Each step jumps to its card.

  1. Dates and flightsMoving a date by a day or two often changes the fare noticeably — compare the days either side first.
  2. eSIMInstall a day or two before you fly, and switch it on at landing instead of roaming.
  3. Airport transferBook ahead for a late arrival, or when the route to your stay is complicated.
  4. Car rentalIf you are hiring a car, fix the pick-up and return times first, and bring the card the deposit will be held on.
  5. Tours and passesThree or more sights? Put a city pass against single tickets before you buy.
  6. LuggageOn a late flight, book storage near your last stop before the airport.
